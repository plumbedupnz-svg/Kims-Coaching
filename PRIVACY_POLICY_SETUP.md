## Put the policy into practice

- Give customers the policy when collecting details in person or through another channel. The website notices cover account/profile forms, private and junior bookings, waitlist requests, and both shop checkout surfaces. They are notices, not blanket consent checkboxes.
- For a player or other person whose information arrives through a parent, another adult, a coach or a club, first establish a permitted basis for indirect collection. Give the affected person an appropriate explanation/link as soon as reasonably practicable, involving a parent where appropriate for the child's circumstances. Record when and how the notice was given, or the evidence supporting an applicable exception. A form asking a parent to share the policy is useful but is not, by itself, proof that the affected person was informed. The website does not add an automated indirect-collection notification system.
- Route access/correction requests and complaints to the privacy officer, check identity/authority proportionately, and track the 20-working-day response deadline. Give reasons and the revised deadline for any lawful extension. Retain a requested statement of correction where required. Do not assume an account holder can receive every record about a child without considering authority and the child's circumstances.
- Review staff access and the arrangements with Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, Resend and any other provider, including overseas processing safeguards. The policy describes obligations; this change does not independently audit provider contracts, production permissions or backup settings.
- Review old player notes, waitlists, profiles, service histories, correspondence and provider logs against their actual purpose; remove or anonymise information that is no longer needed. Keep tax/accounting records for the required period without using that as a reason to keep all other information. No automatic retention or account-deletion job is introduced here.
- Record a customer's request before enabling restring reminders, and action replies asking to change or stop them. Ensure the configured reply-to inbox is monitored. This policy does not turn reminders on or subscribe anyone to marketing.
- Assess privacy incidents promptly and notify the Privacy Commissioner and affected people as soon as practicable if serious harm has occurred or is likely, subject to statutory exceptions. The Act does not impose a universal 72-hour deadline.
